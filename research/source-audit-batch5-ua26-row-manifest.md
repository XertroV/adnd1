# Batch 5 UA p. 26 equipment-table row inventory

This is a source-first, pre-repair inventory of the printed **Armor Class Table** and **Weight and Damage by Weapon Type** on UA p. 26. The input state is frozen commit `679b15d1c580172e54314829c1c3c86dc78b65f3`. No UA p. 26 catalog, equipment field, reference, or review ledger was changed for this inventory.

The authorized original 1985 UA PDF has SHA-256 `e06818cbcce7e4605837ad767e9930c126f43b08d3ce358e14746c7b73eed4f4`; printed page 26 is PDF page 27. I inspected the full page and a zoom of the complete weight/damage table. Its four headers, “Approximate Weight in Gold Pieces” unit, all 68 rows, and “Notes to this table on facing page” cue are present. The weight column is an encumbrance scale, not purchase price. The full Armor Class Table has 11 lines. The facing-page notes are transcribed in the JSON inventory; uncertain marker strings remain unresolved instead of being inferred.

The same page contains a 23-row Basic Equipment and Supplies Costs table, already enumerated separately in `research/price-source-inventory-ua26.json`; those price rows are explicitly excluded as evidence for either table here. The 14-row Weapon Proficiency Table is character-class material and excluded. UA p. 27’s 60-row hand-held weapon table is a separate adjacent table, not a substitute for the p. 26 weight/damage table.

The frozen catalog baseline has 81 UA p. 26 region bindings across 31 equipment catalog files, 42 stable region IDs, and 26 distinct image assets. The binding inventory distinguishes 22 price-region occurrences, 17 basic-costs heading-only occurrences, 17 weight/damage heading-only occurrences, 16 narrow weight/damage row strips without table headings, five full Armor Class table-context bindings, one full weight/damage-table binding, and three other page-26 excerpts whose table topic is not established. These are **binding-occurrence counts**, not unique-row or image counts. The full weight/damage table region `ua-26-92697cf6b5d2d5ec` is bound only to `ua-aklys-hurled`; the full Armor Class crops `ua-26-bce75ca507fdbcda` and `ua-26-22786bf0ad0d5086` contain all 11 armor lines but do not focus individual rows. Price citations and heading-only regions do not count as row evidence.

The row manifest records all 79 source rows and the frozen item/variant matches, including the existing field-key universe and each matched item’s current p. 26 regions. Of 68 weight/damage rows, 65 have at least one confirmed item/context match, two have uncertain targets, and two have no item in the frozen equipment catalog. The uncertain matches are the unqualified Aklys row against a hurled-only record and the Blowgun-and-needle combined row’s component split. The unrepresented rows are Jo Stick and hand quarrel. Light-, medium-, and heavy-horse lance rows remain separate context pointers to generic `phb-lance`; their row facts are not asserted for the generic item. Staff-sling bullet and stone remain distinct rows on one equipment record. Falchion, broad sword, and knife mappings point to the weapon component of records whose names include scabbards.

For Armor Class rows, the rating applies to the printed combination or alternatives, not to standalone armor components. Shield-only mappings remain uncertain because the table does not specify shield type. “Elfin chain mail” remains a distinct variant label without its own frozen item record. The “None” row is a conceptual baseline with no equipment target.

The JSON files preserve the exact SHA-256 and Git blob hashes for `equipment.json`, `citation-overrides.json`, `dist/data.json`, the separate UA p. 26 price inventory, every relevant frozen catalog file, and each image asset. The source-row and catalog-binding denominators are kept separate so a future repair can compare the same frozen state without treating page presence as proof of the right table or field.
