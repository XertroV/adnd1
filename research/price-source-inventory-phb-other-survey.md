# PHB price survey outside the principal equipment tables

The supplied PHB has 130 PDF pages. This preliminary survey excludes printed pages 35–36 (PDF pages 36–37), which have separate inventories. A text-layer locator searched the other 128 PDF pages for cost, price, fee, wage, gp, and gold- or silver-piece terms; 48 PDF pages matched. This is a search denominator, not a count of verified price-bearing pages or proof that all price passages were found. Matches include weight, experience, psionic point costs, and OCR errors such as “cast” read as “cost.”

The following passages were visually inspected in the supplied scan. They are conditional spell or construction costs, and are not ordinary retail price listings:

- Printed p. 69, PDF p. 70: transcription ink costs at least 200 gp per bottle, subject to availability.
- Printed p. 80, PDF p. 81: the specially constructed chest costs at least 5,000 gp; the passage specifies materials and a matching replica.
- Printed p. 83, PDF p. 84: an item prepared for enchantment generally requires raw materials costing more than 100 gp; the passage gives exceptions for certain items.
- Printed p. 86, PDF p. 87: document materials cost 1,000 gp apart from powdered rubies, which cost at least 5,000 gp per document.
- Printed p. 88, PDF p. 89: a specially made ivory likeness has an average cost of 5,000–10,000 gp.

These are source-location findings, not independently reviewed entries. Verify full spell headings, continuation, and conditions before integration. Other promising locator hits occur on printed pp. 20, 22, 25, 29–30, and 32; they remain unreviewed. The remaining matching pages and pages not caught by this limited search remain unreviewed. No source images or OCR transcript are published in this report.
