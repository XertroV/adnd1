# UA 10 and UA 69 crop recipe repair

Four stable catalog region IDs now use complete, already-existing full-context crop recipes from the same authorized UA source pages. The three active defects were captured in the full working-tree baseline before any catalog change: UA p.69 Death Fog `ua-69-29065a55e88e8ad4` clipped the Solid Fog heading, UA p.69 `ua-69-7ceb51358e10f81e` clipped left glyphs and a bottom line, and UA p.10 `ua-10-46ba4e69b0474ea1` clipped right line ends and the Wild Elf ending. The additional UA p.10 left region `ua-10-d3cdc07db7c18a50` was also switched to the complete existing canonical opening recipe so its stable linked-passage pair stays whole.

The pre-fix snapshot `research/source-audit-batch5-active-legacy-crop-baseline.json` covers 3,050 current catalog-region records, including 189 regions not assessable by text geometry. The exact raw old region objects, old crop hashes, old page rectangles, and original private page raster hashes are frozen in `tests/fixtures/continuation-ua10-ua69-crops-before.json`. All four original PNG files remain byte-identical in the catalog directory. No crop was newly generated; the replacement images are existing complete canonical files with exact matching hashes. Focus rectangles, source records, references, mechanics, field grades, and continuation manifest remain unchanged by this repair.

The authorized original UA PDF is identified by SHA-256 `e06818cbcce7e4605837ad767e9930c126f43b08d3ce358e14746c7b73eed4f4`. Printed p.10 is PDF p.11 and p.69 is PDF p.70. Full original-page rasters were reviewed. On p.10, the Wood Elf passage starts at the bottom of the left column and continues at the top of the right column; the complete p.10 crops include that full passage and the Wild Elf entry's ending. On p.69, the Solid Fog entry begins at the bottom of the left column and its complete prose continues at the top of the right column. The repaired Death Fog catalog labels those excerpts as related Solid Fog context, not Death Fog prose.
