> Historical worker proposal, superseded by [the independent integration receipt](source-audit-batch5-conflict-integration.json). Read this document as the preserved pre-integration proposal.

This review dispositions the detect-first set of **22 formal source-conflict fields** and **78 record-caution candidates** captured before source review. The 78 candidates overlap by record and often describe the same source question; they are not 78 independent errors. Each candidate has an individual disposition, source proof, and remaining review need in [`source-audit-batch5-conflicts.json`](source-audit-batch5-conflicts.json).

The source pages were checked against the supplied original PHB, UA, DMG, and Monster Manual scans. PHB p.77’s Magic-user Massmorph header received a 300-dpi re-render, but the first dimension’s unit glyph remains ambiguous between **11′ × 1″** and **11″ × 1″ square per level**. The proposed patch preserves both readings and the source image pending independent adjudication. Its readable creature-capacity rule, “up to 10 … creatures per caster level,” remains in the separate effect statement.

The related Illusionist Massmorph `/fields/Area` value, **1″ × 1″ square per level**, is supported by the distinct p.97 header. That entry says the spell is the same as the fourth-level magic-user spell “except as noted above,” so the printed header is an explicit variant qualifier. The area remains source-checked; a synthetic overwrite to `11″ × 1″` was detected as a stale value by the targeted field audit.

Three existing formal statuses are false conflicts: Plant Growth’s magic-user entry explicitly uses its own square-area header and says it follows the druid spell except as noted; Acrobat’s retained-skill text directly states which skills stop, remain at fifth-level values, or continue improving; and Ceremony’s “indicated or lower” qualifier applies to the caster while Initiation’s 7th-level threshold describes the recipient. The patch bundle removes these three statuses, adds five source-proven formal conflicts, and projects **24** formal source-conflict fields after rebuilding the ledger. The Massmorph glyph status remains open pending independent review.

The five proposed additions are the PHB assassin 14th-level class-limit versus 15th-level advancement conflict; the UA paladin attack-band overlap at 12th level; Speak with Dead’s overlapping 7th-level table rows; Wall of Thorns’ incompatible header, prose, and example volumes; and the unresolved replacement-versus-stacking relationship between Find Familiar’s general HP penalty and the MM special-familiar level loss. The full text and provenance are in the JSON report and structured patch bundle.

All 18 equipment caution candidates received dispositions. The 14 weight, encumbrance, capacity, package-size, and combined-weight notes remain as bounded source qualifiers: the generic DMG entries do not define the PHB subtype, contents, or quantity, and UA reports blowgun-plus-needle weight together. Four PHB purchase-price absences are routed to the price worker, per task scope; no prices are inferred from approximate weapon values.

The rest of the candidate list distinguishes literal source oddities and genuine cross-reference ambiguity from bounded extraction scope, omitted external material, and duplicate notes. Candidate 45 remains with both plausible Massmorph glyph readings; candidate 46 is a duplicate and is proposed for retirement. Candidates 31, 32, 47, and 71 are false Acrobat, Plant Growth, and Ceremony notes. The future candidate count must be regenerated after integration.

The detect-first baseline validates the full 13,418-field ledger against all saved review records. Before fixes, a synthetic downgrade of Mirror Image’s `/exceptions/0` was detected as one missing source-conflict identity, and deleting one caution candidate failed the 78-item denominator check. A targeted Massmorph fixture records that the current p.77 note omits one plausible unit reading, and the p.97 area overwrite fixture detects an unsupported scalar change as stale. These fixtures are preserved in the report and patch bundle so the detector continues to catch accidental loss.

The patch bundle is at `/workspace/scratch/batch5-conflicts/structured-patches.json`. It contains proposed data and review-record edits, all 22 status transitions, the five new formal statuses, and the pre-fix detector fixtures. **No shared data, catalog, gap ledger, or audit ledger was changed by this review.** The baseline and report preserve the pre-integration state.
