# Source-first price inventory: working scope and method

This is a bounded working inventory, not an inventory of every price or a measure of whole-book coverage. Begin with the printed source table, transcribe every row in that table, then compare each row with the current site. Source row count is the denominator for that named table only. Separate rows and variants remain separate; preserve printed package quantities and units. A printed amount, a suggested price, an uncertain interpretation and a price from another edition are distinct claims. Do not infer a source omission from a site name search alone: compare variants, quantities and semantic equivalents before calling a row absent.

Each record identifies the source book, printed and PDF page, source-file SHA-256, the transcribed amount/currency/unit, and normalized full-page row coordinates. Annotations and OCR help locate text, but the original scan is the authority. The source coordinates are working estimates until an independent visual check confirms complete label, value, and any necessary footnote without misleading adjacent rows. No proposed coordinates in these working files are approved for public highlights yet. A footnote outside the row must have separate bounds if a future interface presents it as source evidence.

- `price-source-inventory-dmg28.json`: DMG p.28 standard hireling daily/monthly costs, 10 occupations and 20 rate cells. Monthly quarters and three marked 10% surcharges are explicit; row bounds remain provisional. This is separate from expert hirelings.
- `price-source-inventory-phb35.json`: PHB p.35, armor, arms, clothing and herbs, 80 source rows. All amounts and currencies were independently reviewed; five row bounds were corrected, 42 rows are mapped and 38 remain semantically unresolved. Coordinates require final visual QA before publication. The separate money-changing, banking, loan and jewel transactions are not part of this row count.
- `price-source-inventory-dmg-services-construction.json`: DMG pp.103–104, 106–108 and 115, with 101 rows covering clerical services, construction and siege costs, and suggested receptacle costs, plus seven groups of conditions. Amounts and conditions have been independently checked; bounds remain provisional. Suggested receptacle costs and service fees are distinct from printed equipment purchase prices.

`price-source-inventory-index.json` is an automated text-layer discovery index of candidate pages in the five provided PDFs. It is not a list of all price-bearing pages: text layers can omit or misrecognize material, generic search terms can match unrelated passages, and end-matter identity or availability can vary. Candidate pages are explicitly unreviewed. Beyond the bounded sections above, remaining tables and prose have not been systematically inventoried, including other DMG services, trade, treasure and property and Monster Manual/Monster Manual II valuation and sale references. Those must be reviewed source-first before claiming broad coverage.

For updates: verify the exact source file hash and printed/PDF page; visually inspect the original page; record every row, including conditional or variant prices and citations; compare to semantically equivalent site records; classify unmatched and conflicting rows separately; obtain independent row/bounds review before using highlights. Keep corrections reversible with exact old/new snapshots and historical review evidence. Private scans are evidence and must not be published as a side effect of this inventory.
