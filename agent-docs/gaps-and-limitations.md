# Gaps and limitations

The Sources & coverage page renders `dist/coverage-gaps.json`, generated by `tools/coverage-gaps.mjs` during assembly from `research/gaps-limitations-next-task.json` and the current committed evidence. The projection stays separate from `dist/data.json` so a presentation change does not alter the audited catalog snapshot.

Preserve the distinction between unrepresented offers, unextracted fields, conflicting readings, missing referenced content, unverified pages, recorded cautions and review queues. Their counts may overlap; do not add them into an overall error count. A record link opens the entry and does not establish that every entry reference supports the individual concern. Source-location labels must retain that scope.

The Sling & Bullets dozen offer is separately extracted at 15 sp, alongside the existing 10 sp-per-score bullet offer. The UA “Quarrels for same” amount is separately extracted at 100 gp with quantity unknown; do not infer a per-bolt price. Preserve the older stale/provisional source-inventory assessments as history rather than treating them as current approvals. Arrow's dozen price is extracted separately. Editorial estimates never replace a missing printed price.

Keep unknown continuation, cross-page sequencing, contextual adequacy and fact-level highlighting limitations visible. A successful declared-segment check does not discover unknown continuations. An intentionally unmarked whole-spell exception does not verify individual facts. Publishing a caution does not resolve its underlying source question or improve its review grade.

Before changing a systemic category, capture its complete bounded inventory and preserve representative failures. The pre-page browser and reconciliation snapshots record what was absent; regression tests check the resulting projection and rendered entries. Reject stale input hashes, missing records or changed values rather than silently publishing old assertions. Refresh the handoff from current evidence when the catalog changes; do not copy prior review approval to changed source content.

Run assembly, the Node test suite and the coverage generators. Browser checks in `tests/coverage-gaps-browser.mjs` cover actual rendered entry coverage, status distinctions, record links, error/retry handling, navigation while loading, keyboard access and mobile layouts. Preserve existing source cards, page mappings, private-source notices and source-viewer links.

Declared linked passages are published separately as `dist/source-continuations.json`. Excerpt navigation can traverse the listed excerpts and cited pages; this does not establish that all required source passages have been found. Keep that distinction visible on the limitations page even when a known passage can now be viewed together.

Refresh the handoff with `node tools/refresh-gaps-handoff.mjs` only after assembly and current mechanics, source-scan, systemic, visual and source-table reports validate. The helper preserves the original curated-note denominator and receipt-backed history, while the page displays current active cautions. Supplemental row transcriptions remain separate from the immutable discovery censuses and from independent source/overlay review. A source-specific field candidate may already have an equivalent fact under another source; it is not automatically a missing rule.
