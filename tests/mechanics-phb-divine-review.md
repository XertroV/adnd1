# PHB cleric and druid mechanics review

First batch: 20 cleric records, Bless through Silence, 15′ Radius. The supplied PHB pages 43–45 (PDF pages 44–46; SHA-256 e43e667bad1c2cb093bb28b47f78ec663623b367ae817895bd188bdc3dd4f095) were visually examined, including the same-page column continuation for Hold Person. Existing field values and individual effect, condition, and exception assertions were compared to those passages; no change proposal was established in this batch. The JSON identifies exactly which existing paths were compared. This does not establish exhaustive coverage of all details omitted from a card, of other citations, or of the remaining 134 divine records. Silent or absent fields were not independently verified.

Second batch: 20 cleric records, Slow Poison through Exorcise, visually compared to PHB pp.45–48; related sources PHB pp.40, 43 and 73 were also inspected. The printed text itself shows “1” without a visible range unit for Speak With the Dead and Exorcise; Speak With the Dead's level table also overlaps at seventh level. The p.40 fourth-level list and p.48 third-level description for Detect Lie visibly conflict. Three proposals correct the cards' OCR/visual-review status wording while preserving these unresolved source ambiguities. Cure Blindness's separate illusionist secondary exception was not checked in this batch. These checks do not establish omission completeness.

Third batch: 20 cleric records, Lower Water through Conjure Animals, compared against PHB pp.48–51 and, for shared healing and protection text, pp.43–44. Existing assertions were checked, not all omitted details. No further change proposal was established in this batch.

Fourth batch: 20 records, Find the Path through Faerie Fire, compared with the printed passages through PHB p.54. The Earthquake table explicitly prints structural damage as 5d12; the Holy (Unholy) Word table is visually legible, so its OCR-only warning can be replaced with a direction to the printed table. Wind Walk's passenger-capacity tension is present in the printed text and remains unresolved; its wording can accurately say so. All proposed changes are in the JSON, and no exhaustive omission review is claimed.

Fifth batch: 20 druid records, Invisibility to Animals through Call Lightning, compared to PHB pp.54–57 and relevant cross-referenced passages. The Charm Person or Mammal p.55 renewal-save table agrees with the recorded schedule; its exception no longer needs to say visual confirmation is pending. Spell effects and other existing assertions are checked at the paths reported, without a completeness claim.

Sixth batch: 20 druid records, Cure Disease through Produce Fire, compared to PHB pp.57–60 and their cited shared passages. The p.58 Plant Growth text itself omits the time unit for its listed restricted movement; the proposed note attributes the uncertainty to the printed source rather than OCR. No time unit is invented.

Seventh batch: 20 druid records, Protection from Lightning through Transport Via Plants, compared to PHB pp.60–62 and cited source passages. Direct visual review resolves six previously OCR-withheld values: Repel Insects duration 1 turn/level; Speak With Plants casting time 1 turn; Anti-Plant Shell 16-foot-diameter hemisphere; Commune With Nature casting time 1 turn; Wall of Fire ring radius up to ½″ per level; and Fire Seeds berry burst ½″ diameter. The JSON proposes these exact values and removal of obsolete OCR warnings.

Final batch: 14 records, Turn Wood through the remaining cleric Symbol, compared to PHB pp.62–64 and the relevant source passages. Wall of Thorns total volume remains unresolved: the printed volume statements and the dimensions in the examples conflict, and the proposal attributes that uncertainty to the source rather than to OCR alone. Across all eight batches, 154 records have scoped existing-assertion path checks; this is not an exhaustive source-omission or full mechanics review. Speak With Animals retains its unidentified special-chart note. The only external secondary path explicitly omitted in this pass is the p.95 Cure Blindness exception, recorded unresolved in JSON.

Follow-up: all 154 records' `/class` and `/level` values were compared against the visually inspected class spell tables on PHB pp.40–41 (PDF pp.41–42); all match their respective table. Detect Lie's p.48 description still prints level 3; the distinct spell table says level 4. The Cure Blindness exception was checked against the actual PHB p.95 illusionist Blindness passage: the stated restriction and removal methods agree. It is no longer marked unchecked.

A separately sourced [Acaeum transcription of PHB errata](https://www.acaeum.com/library/errata_phb.html) attributes corrections 48A, 49A and 58A to *Dragon* #35. It reports Detect Lie level 4, cause critical wounds 6–27 points, and Water Breathing components V, S respectively. The magazine itself has not been inspected. The supplied PHB p.58 header prints V, S, M, so any use of the reported Water Breathing correction needs an explicit policy decision and separate provenance; it is not a visual finding from the scanned page. The card's Cure Critical Wounds reverse damage already agrees with the reported correction. The Detect Lie proposal notes both the printed contradiction and the secondary-reported correction.
