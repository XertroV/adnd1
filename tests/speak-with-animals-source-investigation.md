# Speak With Animals: the special reaction chart

## Conclusion

The supplied books do not establish the identity of the “special reaction chart” named in the cleric spell. The **Encounter Reactions** table in the *Dungeon Masters Guide* (DMG) is a plausible related procedure, but the sources examined do not explicitly equate it with the spell's chart. Its heading and scope are general, and it includes hostile results while the spell independently says the animal and others of its kind with it do not attack for the spell's duration. That difference does not prove the general table cannot be adapted to the favor/service decision; it prevents treating the two procedures as demonstrated identical.

## Evidence

Page references below distinguish printed page from one-based PDF page. Each passage listed was rendered from the supplied PDF using PyMuPDF and visually inspected; the inspection concerns the cited passage, not the full accuracy of every field in either spell record.

- **Player's Handbook (PHB), printed p. 46, PDF p. 47, left column, Speak With Animals:** the cleric may communicate with a nonmindless animal. Animals of the same kind accompanying it do not attack while the spell lasts. For a neutral animal or one whose general moral bent matches the cleric's, a favor or service is possible; the referee determines this by a special reaction chart using the cleric's charisma and actions as major factors. The passage gives neither the chart's title nor a page number.
- **PHB p. 55, PDF p. 56, right column, druid Speak With Animals:** except for its stated differences, it refers to the second-level cleric spell. It does not name a chart.
- **DMG p. 41, PDF p. 42, right column, cleric spell commentary:** the animal need not become a true friend; an intelligent hostile animal can be terse or evasive. This passage does not name or locate the special chart.
- **DMG p. 43, PDF p. 44, left column, druid spell commentary:** the same caveat applies to the druid spell; this passage also does not name or locate the chart.
- **DMG p. 63, PDF p. 64, left column, Encounter Reactions:** a general percentile reaction table for an intelligent creature that can be conversed with adjusts the roll for charisma and applicable loyalty and yields reactions from hostile to friendly. The scope and charisma adjustment make it a plausible comparison, but it does not mention Speak With Animals or identify itself as that spell's special chart.
- **PHB p. 13, PDF p. 14, Charisma Table and notes:** the reaction adjustment concerns dealings with encountered persons and creatures. This supports the relevance of charisma, but neither names the spell's chart nor establishes the DMG table as its identity.
- **DMG p. 102, PDF p. 103, lower left, NPC Encounter/Offer Reaction Adjustments:** this table adjusts NPC reactions using personality-related characteristics. Its explicit NPC context does not establish it as the animal spell's chart.

The supplied PHB, DMG, *Unearthed Arcana*, *Monster Manual*, and *Monster Manual II* PDF text layers were searched for “special reaction,” reaction charts/tables/adjustments, and Speak With Animals. OCR can miss or misread text; the absence of a match is not proof that no relevant passage exists. The key PHB spell, both DMG spell comments, and the candidate general table were then checked visually. The search is limited to these supplied editions, not a claim about all published material or authorial intent.

## Supported treatment

Keep the spell's favorable-service determination attributed to the referee and the stated charisma and behavior factors. The existing spell records already express that without naming a numerical chart; no mechanics change is warranted by this investigation. DMG pp. 41 and 43 can be cited as supplementary commentary about the limits of friendliness, with their distinct cleric and druid contexts. If the general DMG p. 63 encounter table is mentioned, label it as a possible general comparison or referee option, not as the identified special chart or a mandatory replacement. Forcing one of its hostile results into this spell without respecting the PHB's no-attack clause would overstate the evidence.

A source that explicitly links the spell's “special reaction chart” to a named table, reproduces that special chart, or clarifies the intended procedure in an authorized edition or authoritative erratum could resolve the identity. No such link was established from the supplied books.
