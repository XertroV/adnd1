# Independent remaining-batches review

The citation integration was compared with the independently reviewed source-page proposals and the pre-integration checkpoint. The assembled 638 spell cards retain all non-reference fields. The 102 added reference objects cover 113 book/page pairs on 80 cards; each added page is supported by an accepted proposal, and every accepted page is accounted for by an addition or an existing primary or related reference. The assembled references match the retained baseline plus the approved additions. New citations remain OCR checked with visual review pending; they do not claim scan verification. No reference repeats a card's primary page or another reference page on the same card.

The seven supplied equipment images were independently inspected. The 29 additional row regions and their overlays were checked against their source crops, including eight newly catalogued items. The integrated catalogues bind the selected values to the relevant image regions. The normal arrow's single and dozen prices retain their quantities; the silver arrow's checked price is for one arrow and its primary source is PHB p.35. The generic arrow row does not establish silver-arrow damage or weight. Matching PHB and UA missile values have one displayed field pair with the other source retained as scoped corroboration. The aklys asterisk remains unexplained, and the atlatl row retains its javelin qualifier.

Read-only checks performed:

- `node --test tests/remaining-batches.test.mjs`: 6 tests passed. These cover all 29 additional rows, item and price quantities, source-grade limits, reference uniqueness and baseline-plus-approved additions, and corroborating missile rows.
- A separate comparison checked every spell's non-reference fields against the checkpoint and compared the approved reference pages in both directions against the integration. No differences or unaccounted approved pages were found. The assembled rules, class data, matrices, source metadata, and progression explainer match the checkpoint; release excerpt counts were updated for the added equipment regions.
- Catalogue integrity checks covered 20 scan-linked items: region counts and verification links resolve, image hashes match, and each recorded PDF page is one greater than its printed page. The dagger entry is unchanged. UA p.26 axe damage and the PHB p.39 range scale retain their OCR-pending status.

The available equipment review is limited to the seven supplied crops. Spell citations were checked in cached OCR, not against printed-page images; these checks do not verify whole books or every mechanic. The supplied full-book hashes were not independently recomputed. The cold threshold for Endure Cold/Endure Heat needs visual review, and whether Mirage Arcane's named comparisons require further references remains open. Three PHB questions also remain open: the special reaction chart for Speak With Animals, a suitable index citation for the class-wide first-level magic-user spells entry, and the symbol version intended by Emotion. No application data was changed by this independent review.
