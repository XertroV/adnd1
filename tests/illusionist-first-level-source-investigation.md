# Illusionist first-level magic-user spells: source investigation

## Finding

The **specific seventh-level spell entry on PHB p.100 lists 20 named first-level magic-user spells and tells the reader to choose from that preceding list**. That printed entry does not include ten other first-level spells in the PHB p.41 magic-user index. However, the introductory note on PHB p.94 says that the seventh-level list includes *all* first-level magic-user spells. The general statement and the specific list do not agree. The p.100 choices are established; a book-wide conclusion that the twenty-name limitation is unambiguous is not. Preserve the conflict for the referee rather than silently expanding or resolving the list.

## Source evidence

- **PHB p.41, PDF page 42:** the magic-user first-level index has 30 numbered spells. The ten in that general index absent from the p.100 list are Dancing Lights, Detect Magic, Find Familiar, Identify, Jump, Light, Push, Spider Climb, Ventriloquism, and Write. Some names also appear as ordinary illusionist spells; that separate occurrence does not itself alter the p.100 selection.
- **PHB p.94, PDF page 95:** the introductory note for illusionist spells describes the seventh-level list as including all first-level magic-user spells and says several are taken as one seventh-level spell. This is a general description, not the p.100 enumerated list. A focused, visually inspected private image and metadata are at the private review archive and the private review archive.
- **PHB p.100, PDF page 101:** the named entry is level 7 and lists Affect Normal Fires, Burning Hands, Charm Person, Comprehend Languages, Enlarge, Erase, Feather Fall, Friends, Hold Portal, Magic Missile, Mending, Message, Nystul’s Magic Aura, Protection from Evil, Read Magic, Shield, Shocking Grasp, Sleep, Tenser’s Floating Disc, and Unseen Servant. Its explanation grants four of the listed first-level magic-user spells at illusionist level 14 and one more for each additional level, requires them to be sought and learned as a magic-user would, and describes choosing this as taking four or more first-level spells as one seventh-level spell. The entry says its starred characteristics are as appropriate to the chosen spell. It does not explain every possible preparation or slot edge case.
- **PHB p.26, PDF page 27:** the class discussion and illusionist spell progression table supply class context, but do not enumerate this selection or reconcile pp.94 and 100.
- **UA p.80, PDF page 81:** the illusionist spell-book section says that, if first-level magic-user spells are known and used, they require a separate book; its capacity is not an expansion of the specific PHB p.100 list. The material inspected does not resolve the p.94/p.100 conflict. This is not a claim that every potential later erratum or source was examined.

The PHB and UA pages above were visually inspected from rendered copies of the supplied PDFs. The PHB source is SHA-256 `e43e667bad1c2cb093bb28b47f78ec663623b367ae817895bd188bdc3dd4f095`. The investigation informed the card and citation changes.

## Proposed card clarification

- Rename the existing allowed-spells field **“PHB p.100 listed spells”** and retain its 20 names.
- Keep the level-14 acquisition and learning text, and add: **“PHB p.100 directs selection from its 20 named spells. The introductory note on p.94 instead says the seventh-level list includes all first-level magic-user spells; the PHB p.41 general index lists 30. The sources conflict, so ask the referee how to apply the broader statement.”**
- Clarify the existing seventh-level entry wording: **“When taking this entry, the PHB describes the four or more selected first-level spells as one seventh-level spell; the entry does not spell out every preparation or slot edge case.”**
- Cite PHB p.100 for the selection and acquisition text, p.94 for the conflicting introduction, and p.41 for the general index comparison. UA p.80 can be cited separately for the spell-book requirement if that requirement is included in the card.
