# Independent class expansion review

Reviewed the private `classes-expanded-next.json` checkpoint read-only on 2026-11-04. The final inspected checkpoint contains 16 guides and 1,039 level-change groups. This is a bounded review of new high-risk material, not a claim that every group or every class rule is complete.

## Sources and checks

Used actual Library OCR from the approved PHB sixth printing and original UA. PHB and UA printed page N maps to PDF page N+1. No source images were available; all visual-verification limitations remain in place.

- Monk, PHB pp. 30–32: weapon damage adds half a hit point per level; surprise decreases to 32% at level 2 and then by two percentage points per level; the post-stun kill term increases above level 7; catalepsy, henchman, and follower formulas match the readable prose. The legible adjacent level-16/17 AC and open-hand damage rows match. Movement and the quivering-palm unlock level remain withheld where headers or table letters are missing.
- Monk thief skills, PHB pp. 28 and 30: only the six expressly inherited skills are used at equal thief level, with readable adjacent rows. The PHB p. 30 versus DMG p. 74 attack-table conflict is explicitly retained.
- Thief-acrobat, UA pp. 23–25: ordinary thief HP, backstab, proficiency, and follower inheritance is qualified by UA p. 25. Only climbing receives future skill increases; retained stealth and language growth remains an explicit ambiguity. Magical writing is excluded.
- Cavalier and UA paladin, UA pp. 14–16: mounted lance damage, saddle protection, and weapon-of-choice attack bonuses match the prose; attack bonuses do not add damage or permit hits requiring magical weapons. The UA paladin inheritance is cited separately. Ability-score advancement preserves “exceeds 100”; roll allocation is left to the source procedure because the operative sentence does not expressly prescribe separate rolls.
- Barbarian, UA pp. 19–21: sampled back protection, illusion detection, magic detection caps, effective Charisma, horde duration, and leader-to-aide half-level changes match the readable passages.
- Ranger, UA pp. 21–22: base tracking gains, final adjusted percentage limit, and the sampled track-identification unlocks match the source. Identification retains its separate roll after successful tracking.
- Bard, PHB pp. 117–118 with p. 21: visible charm, lore, language and college deltas match; the equal-level druid innate powers are expressly supported, while spell casting has its separate level cap. Level 23 adds one fixed HP and reaches level-13 druid casting.
- Cleric, PHB p. 20: level 12 and the clear adjacent 17, 22, 23, 26–29 spell-slot changes match. Druid 5, 11 and 12 match PHB p. 21. Wisdom qualifications remain separate.
- Assassin, PHB pp. 19 and 27–29: effective thief level is assassin level minus two, except backstab. The level-12 scroll unlock is explicitly confirmed by the PHB p. 19 footnote. The ordinary-language reading formula and sampled skill deltas agree with the source.

## Finding corrected during review

UA p. 21 explicitly rounds down the barbarian leader-to-aide half-level but does not explicitly resolve an odd aide level when halving again for assistants. Reported the unsupported certainty in assistant-level deltas. The author removed those deltas and added a pending rounding note; the final checkpoint was checked for their removal.

## Structural result and limits

The inspected expansion leaves XP rows and XP continuation rules unchanged. Every level-change group remains marked incomplete with OCR/visual-review status. No blocking discrepancy remained in the sampled material after the assistant-rounding correction. Numeric saving-throw bands, unreadable skill cells, full spell-slot tables, and other listed gaps remain incomplete. No production class data or UI files were edited by this reviewer.
