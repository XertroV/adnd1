# Emotion: hopelessness symbol investigation

## Finding

The supplied *Player's Handbook* does not identify which class's Symbol spell is meant by the illusionist Emotion spell's reference to a hopelessness symbol. Both the cleric and magic-user spells have a hopelessness option, and the magic-user description supplies additional behavioral detail. The wording alone does not settle whether those additional details apply to Emotion. Treat the two Symbol descriptions as labeled comparisons, not as an established choice of one version.

## Evidence checked in rendered original pages

- PHB printed p. 97 (PDF page 98), Emotion: the hopelessness option refers to the effect of a hopelessness symbol without identifying the spell's class. The spell itself supplies a negating saving throw, says hate and hopelessness counter one another, and says the spell lasts while the illusionist concentrates. The original pixels clearly show **Area of Effect: 4″ × 4″ area**; the PDF's text layer omitted a dimension. This is an actual area, not a range or an assumption about feet.
- PHB printed p. 53 (PDF page 54), seventh-level cleric Symbol: the hopelessness symbol causes creatures that fail their save to turn back in dejection and/or surrender to capture or attack; its stated effects last 3–12 turns. The entry refers the reader to the eighth-level magic-user Symbol, but does not connect the Emotion reference to either class.
- PHB printed p. 91 (PDF page 92), eighth-level magic-user Symbol: its hopelessness version likewise calls for turning back or submitting to opponents' demands on a failed save and specifies 3–12 turns; it additionally gives a 25% chance of no action each round and a 25% chance that those taking action turn back or retire from battle, as applicable. The two versions therefore overlap without being textually identical.
- PHB printed p. 76 (PDF page 77), magic-user Fear: Emotion's fear option references a spell of that name; p. 97 independently specifies its saving throw adjustment. This comparison does not identify which Symbol variant the separate hopelessness option means.

The 3–12 turn duration belongs to each *Symbol* description. Emotion independently specifies its concentration-based duration; the sources inspected do not establish that a 3–12 turn duration automatically applies after or instead of Emotion's concentration. Likewise, Emotion specifies its own save; the comparison does not establish that all symbol activation rules apply to Emotion.

## Other supplied books searched

The searchable text layers of the supplied Unearthed Arcana and Dungeon Masters Guide were searched for `hopelessness` and pages containing both `emotion` and `symbol`. The hits found were an Unearthed Arcana spell-list mention and Heroes' Feast immunity to hopelessness, and an unrelated Dungeon Masters Guide magic-item effect. None resolved the class reference. This is a bounded search of the supplied text layers, not proof that no erratum or other printing exists.

## Supported card and citation treatment

- Add `Area: 4″ × 4″ area` to Emotion and remove the OCR-related area-withholding exception.
- Keep PHB 97 as the primary Emotion source and PHB 76 as the Fear comparison. Identify PHB 53 and PHB 91 explicitly as **cleric Symbol comparison** and **magic-user Symbol comparison**, respectively; neither is confirmed as the intended one.
- Preserve the class-neutral description that hopelessness refers to a symbol's effect and the card's own concentration duration. Do not silently add the magic-user's 25% behavior or either Symbol's 3–12 turn duration as settled Emotion mechanics.
